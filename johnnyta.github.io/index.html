I am a first-generation student from Charlotte, North Carolina pursuing a degree in Neuroscience on the pre- medicine track and hope to specialize in pediatric oncology. My interest in medicine stems from my interests in the sciences and my passion to serve those in underserved communities.

A community that stands close to my heart is the Cambodian community in North Carolina. As a youth ambassador for the Cambodian Legacy Project: a cultural organization preserving Cambodian culture in North Carolina, I work with youth populations to preserve and expand Khmer culture. With cultural programs such as our Cambodian language class, I taught students the basics of how to read and write in Khmer, the language of Cambodia. Furthermore, community events, such as health clinics and citizenship workshops, have allowed me to connect with and serve those in my community.

Additionally, as a youth volunteer at a local hospital, I guided and transported patients from to the departments of their appointments. Through this experience, I gained invaluable patient experience that changed the way I viewed the patient side of healthcare. I plan to continue building on this experience by continuing to volunteer during my time at Carolina, whether it be in healthcare or in public service, I want to continue serving my community.

Outside of school I enjoy creating short and long form personal video projects, including producing a long-form video documentary about my senior capstone project. Additionally, I also love solving Rubik’s cubes and other twisty puzzles in my free time. I speed-solve competitively, currently holding the 19th fastest single solve in North Carolina, and I hope to continue competing throughout my time at Carolina!
