04/05 - The little things

Writing is hard. I would argue that one of the greatest abilities one can have is the ability to truly express oneself 1:1 through a medium. It is an ability that unfortunately not many people will ever be able to do. I have tried many mediums to express myself: writing, video, and music, and sometimes I worry that I may never be able to use any of them to express myself well. I find that I hold myself back largely due to a fear of how I will be perceived, and as a result I never allow myself to be perceived. The other day I was eating dinner with my family and my parents asked me:

what do you think your greatest weakness is?

Maybe my greatest weakness is my tendency to get caught up on the little things.
